WhiteWater Rafting

Whitewater Rafting Adventures (WRA) was founded with a simple but powerful mission: to help people experience the thrill, beauty, and adventure of nature through safe, world-class river rafting trips. The company started as a small, passion-driven outdoor guiding service created by a group of experienced river enthusiasts who believed that whitewater rivers were more than just waterways—they were opportunities for challenge, teamwork, and unforgettable memories.

History

WRA began operations in 2010, offering guided rafting tours on a single stretch of Class II–III river. With just two rafts, minimal gear, and big ambition, the company built its reputation on exceptional safety practices, skilled guides, and a personalized adventure experience.
